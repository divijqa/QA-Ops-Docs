# QA Risk Management Guide

QA identifies, assesses, mitigates, tracks, and communicates risk across the release lifecycle.

## Practice

Assess product, technical, process, and operational risk by severity and likelihood. Assign every material risk an owner, mitigation plan, and status. Accepted risks are documented, approved, included in sign-off, and monitored after release.
