# QA Communication Protocol

QA communication is clear, timely, consistent, transparent, and owned.

## Protocol

Use agreed channels and report daily progress, defects, blockers, environment health, regression status, and release readiness. Critical blockers are communicated within 15 minutes with impact, severity, evidence, owner, and required action.
