# QA SLA and KPI Definitions

SLAs define minimum performance expectations. KPIs measure QA quality, efficiency, and delivery effectiveness.

## Core measures

- Test execution and reporting timeliness
- Defect logging and retest turnaround
- Release sign-off timeliness
- Coverage, defect density, and defect leakage
- Automation coverage, MTTD, MTTV, and environment stability

Review measures quarterly, track trends in dashboards, and share results across QA, Engineering, Product, and Operations.
