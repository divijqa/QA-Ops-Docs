# Hotfix Workflow

The Hotfix Workflow allows urgent production fixes to move quickly without abandoning quality governance.

## Flow

Validate the issue and its critical impact, triage a constrained scope, develop and review the fix, run smoke and targeted regression, document risk, secure QA, Engineering, Product, and Operations approval, validate in production, then complete RCA and improvement actions.
