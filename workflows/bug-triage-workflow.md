# Bug Triage Workflow

Bug triage aligns QA, Engineering, and Product on impact, urgency, owner, and target resolution.

## Triage outcomes

Every reviewed defect has reproducible evidence, severity, priority, component tagging, an owner, dependencies, a target version, and an agreed follow-up path. Blockers and high-severity issues are communicated immediately.
