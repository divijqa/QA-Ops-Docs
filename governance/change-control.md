# Change Control

Change Control ensures changes to code, environments, configurations, processes, and release scope are documented, reviewed, approved, tested, and traceable.

## Flow

Record the request and impact, approve it at the right level, implement with QA notification, validate through smoke and targeted regression, update documents, sign off, and retain the audit trail.
