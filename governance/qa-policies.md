# QA Policies

QA work follows documented processes, uses approved tools, maintains traceable records, tests critical flows, and reports honestly and promptly.

## Policy areas

Test execution, defect management, environment readiness and security, release entry and exit criteria, communication, audit readiness, and continuous improvement.
