# Bug Triage Checklist

- [ ] New defects include reproducible steps, evidence, and environment details.
- [ ] Impact, severity, priority, component, dependencies, and target version are agreed.
- [ ] The correct owner is assigned and critical issues are escalated.
- [ ] Triage decisions and verification plans are documented.
