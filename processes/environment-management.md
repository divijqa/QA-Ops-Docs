# Environment Management

Environment Management provides stable, secure, predictable conditions for quality validation.

## Operating expectations

QA validates deployments, services, APIs, data, integrations, logs, accounts, access, and feature flags before testing starts. Refreshes follow an agreed strategy, deployment changes follow change control, and environment issues are logged, communicated, and verified through resolution.
