# Release Readiness Process

Release readiness establishes whether quality, stability, risk, and compliance evidence supports deployment.

## Decision criteria

- Scope, acceptance criteria, environments, and test data are ready.
- Functional, regression, smoke, exploratory, and applicable automated testing are complete.
- Critical and high defects are resolved or explicitly accepted.
- Risks, artifacts, rollback approach, and monitoring plan are documented.
- QA, Engineering, Product, and Operations complete the go/no-go review.
