# QA Test Lifecycle

The end-to-end operating model for planning, executing, validating, and reporting quality work.

## Stages

1. Test intake: requirements, scope, risks, and estimates.
2. Planning: strategy, data, environments, and responsibilities.
3. Design: traceable test cases mapped to requirements and risk.
4. Preparation: access, tools, environment, and test data readiness.
5. Execution: smoke, functional, regression, and exploratory testing.
6. Defect management: log, triage, verify, and close.
7. Reporting: progress, coverage, quality signals, and risks.
8. Release readiness: evidence-based sign-off.
9. Retrospective: learnings converted into process improvements.
